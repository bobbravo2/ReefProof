# Code of conduct

ReefProof contributors must make participation respectful, safe, and welcoming.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information,
and unsafe testing against living systems are not acceptable.

Project discussions should address the work and its evidence. Assume good faith,
give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and respect maintainers'
decisions about safety and release scope. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to
protect contributors or users.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact
or private vulnerability-reporting channel. Do not publish sensitive personal or
system information while requesting help.
